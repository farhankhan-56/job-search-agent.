Job Search Agent

Find jobs that fit you

Upload your CV or type a role. The agent searches several job boards, removes suspicious posts and ranks what is left by how well it fits you.

  • Every job gets a trust score, and posts that ask applicants for money are removed.
  • Every job gets a match score with a short reason, so you can start with the best fits.
  • The full list is saved to a Google Sheet you can share.
